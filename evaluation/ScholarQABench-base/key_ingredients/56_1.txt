By focusing on the most representative samples, active learning minimizes the effort required for data annotation and enhances the efficiency of the model training process. For example, in the context of 6G networks, active learning frameworks can optimize both data acquisition and annotation, leading to improved computational efficiency and adaptability in network intelligence[1]."
